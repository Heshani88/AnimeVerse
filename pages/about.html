About AnimeVerse

Welcome to AnimeVerse — a place where anime fans can discover, explore and organize their favorite anime.

AnimeVerse is a responsive web application developed using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It provides an easy-to-use interface for exploring anime series and discovering new favorites.

🎌 Explore Anime

Browse a collection of anime and discover new series from different genres.

🔍 Search & Filter

Search for anime and filter the collection by genre to quickly find what you want.

❤️ Favorites

Save your favorite anime and access them easily whenever you want.

📋 Watchlist

Create a personal watchlist and keep track of anime you want to watch.

📺 Popular Shows

Explore popular anime shows and discover series you may enjoy.

🎭 Anime Genres

Explore anime by genres such as Action, Adventure, Fantasy and more.
